# MEMO Life Studio — Game Prototype

MEMO Life Studio is the game branch of the MEMO lifestyle universe.

## Product Direction

A premium 2.5D lifestyle simulation game centered on a frequently travelling girl who:
- decorates her home and Mediterranean courtyard
- changes outfits and packs for trips
- paints and runs a creative side hustle
- cares for and bonds with her cat
- receives and sends postcards
- develops mood, healing, cultivation and simplified feng shui states
- connects with friends through social and UGC systems

Players can either role-play as the heroine or participate as friends around multiple girls.

## Current Prototype

Base44 app: **Memo Home: 治愈时光**

Editor:

The Base44 version is the first playable web prototype. It is intentionally 2.5D and modular so the content/data layer can later support lightweight Blender/GLB assets, mobile and Steam builds.

## Core Modules

1. Home + semi-open room
2. Courtyard / garden / pool
3. Dress-up + wardrobe
4. Travel preparation + postcards
5. Cat care / growth / interaction
6. Painting / creative side hustle
7. Mood / healing / cultivation
8. Simplified feng shui
9. Social / friends / visiting
10. UGC outfit and home design

## Visual Rule

The master art direction is the bright Mediterranean MEMO lifestyle image: white architecture, sea, warm sunlight, bougainvillea, cream/pink/lilac/pool-blue accents, fashion-editorial feeling, girl + cat + home + travel.

Feature UI references define layout only; they must not override the master visual atmosphere.
