# MEMO Life Studio — Art Direction

## Master Visual

**Priority:** master atmosphere first, feature-screen layout second.

### World
- Mediterranean seaside home
- semi-open dollhouse rooms
- balcony + courtyard + flowers + pool
- blue sea and clear sky visible from the home
- warm late-morning sunlight
- white / cream architecture
- bougainvillea and abundant but controlled greenery

### MEMO Palette
- Archive Pink
- Rose Pink
- Lilac
- Pool Blue
- Mint
- Butter Yellow
- Cream
- Soft Coral
- Champagne
- black line-work only as an accent / cat signature

### Tone
- premium feminine lifestyle
- airy
- editorial
- playful, not childish
- cozy, not generic cozy-game
- slightly flirty
- self-expressive
- healing without becoming a meditation app

## 2.5D Production Style

The first web version should feel dimensional without requiring a heavy real-time 3D scene.

Use:
- layered foreground / midground / background
- parallax
- soft depth-of-field
- simple ambient motion
- semi-open architecture
- characters and cat in front of scene planes
- interactive hotspots
- high-quality UI glass/cards over the environment

Future 3D assets should remain intentionally light:
- modular Blender sources
- GLB export
- low-poly / mid-poly silhouettes
- reusable materials
- LOD-friendly
- suitable for browser, mobile and Steam

## Character

Main heroine:
- frequent business traveller
- creative side hustle
- paints and sketches
- fashion-aware
- warm, independent and expressive

The character should feel adult and stylish rather than chibi or childlike.

## Cat

Grey-blue / soft grey companion cat.
The cat is a real behavioral system, not a decorative prop.

Visual behavior:
- nap
- stretch
- follow
- sit on furniture
- inspect luggage
- watch painting
- play in garden
- react to mood

## UI

Feature screens may use:
- MEMO serif / fashion-editorial headings
- handwritten annotations
- rounded translucent cards
- restrained pink accent
- cream glass panels
- small line icons
- generous white space

Avoid:
- dense mobile-app dashboards
- neon
- toy-like buttons
- generic pink casual-game UI
- excessive stickers
- overly dark UI
