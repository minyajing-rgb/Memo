# MEMO Campaign System

## Master Message
**Wear Your Good Mood.**

## Campaign Families

### 1. Good Mood Anywhere
Home / city / work / travel / resort.

### 2. Business → Bar
One inner layer + one polished outer layer.
Remove the jacket, change the shoe or bag, and the look changes mood.

### 3. MEMO Travel Lighter
Packing system, 3–5 outfits, one trip.
Focus on luggage, pouches, accessories, sleep, meeting, city and after-hours.

### 4. Pool Good Mood Club
Colorful swim + resortwear.
Real movement: walk, sit, swim, talk, drink, dance.

### 5. Yacht / Garden / Club
Half business, half leisure.
Women and men together.
Soft tailoring + silk + knit + resort details.

### 6. MEMO Men
Mediterranean-inspired young menswear:
- soft tailoring
- knit polos
- scarves
- resort shirts
- pleated trousers
- textured jackets
- subtle cat embroidery / hardware

### 7. Fortune Edition
Peach blossom / love / luck / abundance collectible visual world.
Use:
- gold rune-like frame
- peach blossom tree
- charms
- crystals / talisman shapes
- Joyce handwritten signature
- love / fortune iconography
- limited card / poster / charm system

## Photography Direction
- bright, editorial, premium
- clean backgrounds or controlled lifestyle environments
- natural movement
- avoid over-posed sensuality
- show garment design first
- full look + close-up details
- multiple people for lifestyle and resort
- color mix instead of same-color total looks

## Styling Rule
Every campaign outfit should contain:
- 1 hero silhouette idea
- 1 material idea
- 1 MEMO detail
- optional 1 accessory story

Never stack all signatures on one look.
